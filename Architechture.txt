TradePlanner Architechture Overview


app.py
│
├── ui_trade.py
│     ├── logic.get_price()
│     ├── logic.plan_trade()
│     └── risk_engine.{compute_basic_perf, compute_adjustments}
│
├── ui_perf.py
│     ├── risk_engine.compute_drawdown (via app.py perf dict)
│     └── logic.market_intel_fetchers
│          ├── fetch_fear_greed
│          ├── fetch_lunarcrush_sentiment
│          ├── fetch_farside_etf
│          ├── fetch_tradingview_indicators
│          ├── fetch_whale_alert_activity
│          ├── fetch_coinglass_exchange_flows
│          └── fetch_news_events
│
├── ui_regime.py
│     └── uses st.session_state.regime (RegimeResult-like object)
│
├── ui_history.py
│     └── uses st.session_state.trades (list of trade dicts)
│
└── ui_docs.py
      └── static documentation

risk_engine.py
│
├── compute_basic_perf(trades)
├── compute_drawdown(trades, start_balance)
└── compute_adjustments(perf, trades, balance, base_risk)

logic.py
│
├── get_price(symbol)
├── plan_trade(...)
└── all market-intel fetchers

Data flow:

Trades live in st.session_state.trades

Balance lives in st.session_state.balance

Risk engine reads trades + perf + balance → returns recommended risk

UI modules are pure views over session_state + engine outputs
